About Vijaya Premix

Your shortcut to authentic Indian cooking.

Our Story

Vijaya Premix started with a simple problem: real Indian cooking takes real time, and most weeknights don't have much of it. Long ingredient lists and multi-step spice blends often mean reaching for takeout instead of the stove.

So we do the complicated part — sourcing, measuring and blending the masala — and pack it into a single premix. You add the fresh ingredients your kitchen already has, and follow a short recipe. The result is food that tastes like it took hours, in a fraction of the time.

Our current range includes two vegetarian gravy premixes and five non-vegetarian chicken and fish premixes for convenient home cooking.

Mission

Make authentic Indian cooking achievable on any night of the week, without asking anyone to compromise on flavour.

Vision

A Vijaya Premix in every kitchen that wants home-cooked food, whatever the schedule looks like.

Our Promise

Authentic taste, less effort, and a spice base you can trust to taste the same, pack after pack.

Why Vijaya

Wide veg & non-veg variety, straightforward recipes, and packs sized for real Indian households.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Vijaya Premix take to cook?

Most premixes are ready in 15–40 minutes depending on the dish — check the pack for exact timing, or the matching recipe in our Recipe Book.

Do I need to add fresh ingredients?

Yes. Vijaya Premix supplies the spice base and masala; most recipes ask for a few fresh ingredients like vegetables, meat, paneer or curd to complete the dish.

Are Vijaya Premixes available in both veg and non-veg?

Yes. Our range spans vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, clearly labelled on every pack and product card.
